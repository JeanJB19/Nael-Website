What I Offer

01

MERL Systems Architecture

Building infrastructure from pre-proposal strategy through full project implementation.

Standard Delivery
  • • MERL Design & Strategy: Authoring MERL strategies aligned directly with proposal requirements and donor-client guidelines.
  • • Frameworks: Elaborating Theories of Change (TOC), Logic Models, LogFrames, MERL plans, and Indicator Tracking Tables (ITT).
  • • Data Architecture: Developing routine data tools, mapping multi-tiered data flows, scoping relational databases, and writing SQL queries.
  • • Data Visualization: Designing BI dashboards to track routine metrics and streamline reporting.
The Tech Edge

AI-Driven Querying & Automated Reporting: Integrating AI-assisted data query tools and automated workflows into report-writing processes to accelerate timelines.

LLM-Powered BI Dashboard: Allowing stakeholders to ask conversational questions (Text-to-SQL) and retrieve instant, dynamic insights.

02

Study & Research Engineering

Structuring rigorous field data collection, mixed-method evaluations, and advanced research protocols.

Standard Delivery
  • • Secondary Evidence & Gap Analysis: Critical literature synthesis, online desk reviews, and information gap mapping.
  • • Protocol Design: Authoring Scopes of Work (SOWs), study question refinement, sampling determination, Data Analysis Plans, and ethical guardrails.
  • • Field Tooling & Manuals: Developing quantitative questionnaires, qualitative guides, enumerator manuals, and synthetic datasets.
  • • Analytical Scripting: Pre-scripting data analysis workflows to guarantee smooth survey execution.
The Tech Edge

Advanced Mobile Survey Engineering: Dynamic mobile data tools featuring complex skip logic, cascading selects, and validation constraints.

Real-Time Data Quality Assessment (DQA): Automated DQA scripts detecting logical errors, outliers, missing data, and enumerator bias in real time.

03

Quant, Qual & Geospatial Analysis

Transforming multi-source data into static & dynamic visual, statistical evidence, and qualitative synthesis.

Standard Delivery
  • • Quant Analysis & ML: Data processing, cleaning, advanced statistical analysis, predictive modeling, and supervised/unsupervised ML.
  • • Qual & NLP Processing: Digitizing field notes, using Natural Language Processing (NLP) & computerized assistance for transcript insight extraction.
  • • Geospatial Analytics: Achievement mapping and spatial data analysis.
  • • Reporting & Data Storytelling: Transforming findings into visual reports and executive briefings designed for evidence-based decision-making.
The Tech Edge

Automated Data Pipelines: Streaming mobile survey data directly into R and Python workflows via custom APIs to drive real-time Power BI dashboards.

AI Literature Synthesis: Grounded AI research tools transforming high-volume document repositories into evidence-based insights.

04

Client Customized Solutions

Tailored digital tools, automated workflows, and systems engineering for specialized operational needs.

Standard Delivery
  • • Lightweight Business Apps: Custom internal web apps, interactive forms, and utility tools built for specific operational tasks without unnecessary software bloat.
  • • End-to-End Task Automation: Automating data workflows using custom scripts, webhooks, and modern automation tools.
  • • System & Data Pipeline Integration: Seamlessly connecting disparate platforms, cloud databases, and field tools to eliminate manual data transfers.
  • • Workflow Audit & Digital Modernization: Evaluating legacy processes to identify operational bottlenecks and replace fragmented workflows with lean digital tools.
  • • Maintenance & Technical Onboarding: Providing hands-on documentation, code walkthroughs, and post-deployment support to ensure long-term adoption and smooth handoffs.
The Tech Edge

Custom Scripting & Middleware: Developing specialized Python, R, and API scripts to extend the capability of off-the-shelf software and solve unique client bottlenecks.

No-Code/Low-Code Automation: Building n8n and webhook-driven workflows that connect field tools, cloud storage, and reporting platforms without heavy engineering overhead.
